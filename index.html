Constitutional Basis: Section 9(2) Equality Clause

Under South African jurisprudence, affirmative action and redress legislation are explicitly governed by Section 9(2) of the 1996 Constitution:

"To promote the achievement of equality, legislative and other measures designed to protect or advance persons, or categories of persons, disadvantaged by unfair discrimination may be taken."

In Minister of Finance v Van Heerden (2004), the Constitutional Court unanimously established that remedial measures are not an exception to equality, but an integral component of achieving substantive equality in post-apartheid South Africa. The viral categorization of non-penal procurement preferences as "oppressive penal race laws" collapses under direct legal scrutiny.
