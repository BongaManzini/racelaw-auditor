# ⚖️ The Law Auditor: South African Legislative Fact-Checker

> **Live Application**: [https://bongamanzini.github.io/racelaw-auditor/](https://bongamanzini.github.io/racelaw-auditor/)

An interactive legal informatics audit engine cross-referencing viral online lists claiming *"145 race-based laws"* against the official *Republic of South Africa Government Gazette* and Constitutional jurisprudence.

---

## 🎯 The Informatics & Civic Verification Angle

- **The Viral Narrative**: Disinformation campaigns frequently circulate lists alleging that South Africa has "over 140 discriminatory race laws" designed to penalize minority citizens.
- **The Informatics Solution**: A structured digital auditor that maps cited statutes to official Government Gazette notices, distinguishing between:
  - 🟢 **Constitutional Redress (Section 9(2))**: Non-penal affirmative action and procurement frameworks.
  - 🔴 **Repealed Apartheid Statutes**: Laws abolished prior to 1994 (e.g., Natives Land Act, Group Areas Act, Population Registration Act) erroneously counted as modern active laws.
  - 🟡 **Subordinate Regulations & Sector Charters**: Industry-specific guidelines without penal sanctions.
  - ⚪ **Universal Non-Racial Statutes**: Routine regulatory frameworks (e.g., corporate beneficial ownership anti-money laundering laws required by FATF).

---

## 🏛️ Key Constitutional Context

Affirmative action in South Africa is grounded in **Section 9(2) of the 1996 Constitution**:
> *"To promote the achievement of equality, legislative and other measures designed to protect or advance persons, or categories of persons, disadvantaged by unfair discrimination may be taken."*

In *Minister of Finance v Van Heerden (2004)*, the Constitutional Court established that remedial measures are not an exception to equality, but an essential mechanism to achieve substantive equality.

## 🛠️ Features

- **Live Search & Filter**: Instant filtering by status (Redress, Repealed, Regulation, Universal) and keyword.
- **Gazette Verification Modal**: In-depth analysis of each Act, comparing the viral claim against its true statutory text.
- **Audit Analytics**: Real-time breakdown metrics of all 145 audited statutes.
